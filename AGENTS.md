# Project working agreement

- The human creator owns the final design decision and intended player experience.
- Do not blindly implement a proposed mechanic. First identify its purpose, risks, alternatives, and trade-offs.
- Clearly distinguish fact, evidence, design hypothesis, recommendation, and speculation.
- The first playable question is whether limited time creates meaningful tension between learning more and acting now.
- The player advises; the protagonist retains agency and must explain acceptance, adaptation, delay, or refusal.
- Score the quality of entrepreneurial inquiry, evidence, risk control, and revision—not a supposedly ideal life path.
- Specific opportunities can expire, but broad life paths should normally remain re-enterable.
- Keep simulation, content, UI, and tests separate. Prefer data-driven content.
- Every meaningful change must preserve or improve the playable core loop and include proportionate verification.
- Do not expand into 3D, open world, combat, multiple protagonists, or runtime generative AI before the core loop is validated with real users.
